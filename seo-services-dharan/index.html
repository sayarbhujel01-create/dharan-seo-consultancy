Frequently Asked Questions About SEO Services in Dharan

What makes professional SEO services in Dharan different from running Facebook boosts?

Paid social boosts stop generating visibility the moment you pause ad spend and interrupt passive scrollers. Professional SEO captures active commercial intent when prospects in Dharan, Sunsari, or across Nepal explicitly search Google for your service or product, compounding in value over time.

How long does it take to see measurable progress from SEO in Nepal?

Technical indexing fixes and Google Business Profile refinements can improve crawl visibility and local discovery within weeks. Sustainable competitive rankings for commercial service terms typically build over 3 to 6 months depending on domain history, market competition, and execution velocity.

Do I need a brand-new website before starting SEO services?

Not necessarily. Most existing WordPress, Shopify, Laravel, React, or static websites can be audited and improved without a costly rebuild. We only recommend structural rebuilding when severe platform limitations block crawlability, mobile usability, or core conversion flows.

Will I retain full ownership of my domain, content, and Google Search Console account?

Yes. You keep 100% ownership of your website, Google Search Console, Google Analytics, Google Business Profile, and every published page or asset created during our engagement.

How do you measure SEO success beyond keyword positions?

We track verified non-branded organic clicks and impressions in Google Search Console, qualified organic landing page sessions, Google Business Profile calls and direction clicks, WhatsApp inquiries, and consultation form submissions.
