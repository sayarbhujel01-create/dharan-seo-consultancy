NAP Consistency & Local Citations Guide for Dharan, Itahari & Biratnagar

Direct Summary (Centerpiece Answer)

NAP consistency means presenting the exact same business Name, physical Address, and Phone number across your website, Google Business Profile, social profiles, and Nepal directories—eliminating entity confusion so search engines trust your local location data.

Supports Core Service: Local SEO Services in Dharan · Published by Dharan SEO Consultancy, Dharan, Nepal · Updated 2026-11-23

Why Nepal Addresses Commonly Break NAP Consistency

In Nepal, street naming varies across wards, chowks, and transliterations (for instance, writing “Dharan-12, Sunsari” on Facebook, “Putali Line, Dharan 56700” on a website, and listing an old landline on an outdated directory). While humans figure it out, inconsistent strings weaken machine confidence in your local entity.

Step-by-Step Citation Hygiene Workflow

  1. Define One Canonical NAP Format: Standardize exact spelling, ward number, postal code (e.g., 56700 for Dharan), and primary contact number.
  2. Align Owned Assets First: Match your website header/footer, Contact page, LocalBusiness JSON-LD schema, Google Business Profile, Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn.
  3. Audit & Update Nepal Directories: Claim or correct listings across reputable Nepal business directories, chamber listings, and industry-specific portals (healthcare, hospitality, education).

Frequently Asked Questions

Are hundreds of low-quality global directories helpful for Local SEO in Nepal?

No. Quality and relevance beat sheer quantity. Focus on accurate major platforms, reputable Nepal directories, and niche-specific local associations.

What should I do if my business moved to a new ward in Dharan?

Immediately update your website, schema markup, Google Business Profile, social profiles, and every existing directory citation so old addresses do not linger.
