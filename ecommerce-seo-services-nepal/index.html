Section 16

Frequently Asked Questions About E-commerce SEO in Nepal

Should an online store in Nepal focus on category pages or individual product pages first?

Both matter, but collection/category pages usually capture higher-volume commercial searches (such as “trekking boots Nepal” or “wireless earbuds price in Nepal”), while product pages capture high-converting model-specific and price queries. A healthy store optimizes both layers cleanly.

How do you handle out-of-stock or discontinued products without losing SEO value?

Temporarily out-of-stock products should remain live with HTTP 200 status, clear availability updates, and related alternatives. Permanently discontinued products with backlinks or traffic should be 301 redirected to the closest replacement product or parent category rather than left as dead 404s.

Why do faceted filters (color, size, price sort) hurt e-commerce SEO?

Uncontrolled filter combinations can generate thousands of near-duplicate parameter URLs that waste crawl budget and dilute authority across your catalog. We apply strict canonical, robots, and selective indexation rules so only high-demand category combinations get indexed.

Which platform is better for SEO in Nepal: Shopify or WooCommerce?

Both can rank strongly when configured properly. WooCommerce gives deeper control over URLs, hosting, and Nepal payment gateways (eSewa, Khalti, Fonepay), while Shopify offers reliable infrastructure and clean checkout speed with certain URL constraints we engineer around.

Can Product schema display NPR prices and stock status in Google Search?

Yes. Properly validated Product and Offer JSON-LD markup using NPR currency (priceCurrency: "NPR"), availability status, and authentic aggregate ratings makes product listings eligible for enhanced rich results in Google Search and Google Shopping surfaces.
