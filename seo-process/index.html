Our Step-by-Step SEO Methodology & Quality Standards

No black-box tactics or vague monthly checklists. Every engagement follows a transparent six-phase workflow rooted in technical crawl integrity, semantic topical authority, and measurable lead tracking.

  1. Commercial Discovery & KPI Alignment: Before inspecting tools, we document your highest-margin services, geographic markets (Dharan, Sunsari, Morang, Koshi Province, or nationwide Nepal), customer decision cycles, and conversion channels (WhatsApp, phone, forms, or checkout).
  2. Full Technical & Indexation Audit: We crawl every URL, inspect Google Search Console coverage, benchmark Core Web Vitals on mobile connections, and map crawl traps, redirect chains, canonical errors, and schema gaps. See Technical SEO Services Nepal.
  3. Search Intent & Topical Map Architecture: We build a clean parent-child blueprint assigning each commercial keyword cluster to one dedicated money page and mapping supporting informational guides to eliminate cannibalization. See On-Page SEO Services Nepal.
  4. Core Commercial & Local Entity Execution: We implement technical fixes, refine commercial landing pages with centerpiece answers and visual semantics, deploy interconnected JSON-LD schema, and harmonize Google Business Profile & NAP citations. See Local SEO Services in Dharan.
